# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: November 15, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

This policy applies to **8 AM Class (我上早八)**, the Connect IQ app for Garmin Forerunner 255. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [project's issue page](https://github.com/springbear521-art/8am-class/issues). Do not include personal health data in a public issue.

本政策适用于 Garmin Forerunner 255 的 Connect IQ 应用 **8 AM Class（我上早八）**。隐私问题可通过[项目反馈页面](https://github.com/springbear521-art/8am-class/issues)联系开发者。请勿在公开问题中附上个人健康数据。

## Data the app uses / 应用使用的数据

While the app is running, it reads the watch clock, motion sensor data, available heart-rate readings, device status such as Do Not Disturb and battery level, and the configured sleep and wake times in the Garmin user profile. Motion and heart-rate information is used to decide whether to alert within the 30-minute early-wake window. The configured wake time is used only if you choose to import it. The app does not read Garmin's recorded sleep stages or actual sleep duration.

应用运行时会读取手表时间、动作传感器数据、可用的心率读数、勿扰模式和电量等设备状态，以及 Garmin 用户资料中配置的睡眠和起床时刻。动作和心率用于判断是否在最晚起床时间前 30 分钟内提前提醒。只有你选择导入时，应用才会将配置的起床时刻用作闹钟。应用不读取 Garmin 记录的睡眠分期或实际睡眠时长。

## Data stored on the watch / 保存在手表上的数据

The app saves the alarm time, language and alert preferences, recent session summaries, and up to 128 recent diagnostic events in its local Connect IQ storage. Diagnostic events can include timestamps, heart-rate readings or baselines, battery and device status, sensor summaries, and alert trigger reasons. If you manually create a matching log file in `GARMIN/APPS/LOGS`, the watch may also write diagnostic text to that file and rotate it to a `.BAK` file. These records describe app monitoring and alert behavior; they are **not** measured sleep duration.

应用会在 Connect IQ 的本地存储中保存闹钟时间、语言与提醒偏好、最近的运行摘要和最多 128 条诊断事件。诊断事件可能包含时间、心率读数或基线、电量与设备状态、传感器摘要及提醒触发原因。如果你手动在 `GARMIN/APPS/LOGS` 创建同名日志文件，手表也可能向其中写入诊断文本，并轮转为 `.BAK` 文件。这些记录描述的是应用监测和提醒过程，**不是**测得的睡眠时长。

## Sharing and retention / 共享与保留

The app has no network connection, advertising, or analytics service and does not automatically send data to the developer or another party. Data remains on the watch unless you choose to export or share a log. The app keeps at most 128 events in local storage; optional text logs rotate according to the watch's Connect IQ logging behavior. To remove diagnostic data, delete the app's stored data and any matching `.TXT` and `.BAK` files in `GARMIN/APPS/LOGS`. Before sharing a log, check it for personal information, including heart-rate readings and times.

应用没有网络连接、广告或分析服务，也不会自动向开发者或其他方发送数据。除非你自行导出或分享日志，数据留在手表上。本地存储最多保留 128 条事件；可选文本日志按手表的 Connect IQ 日志机制轮转。要删除诊断数据，请清除应用的本地数据，并删除 `GARMIN/APPS/LOGS` 中相应的 `.TXT` 和 `.BAK` 文件。分享日志前，请检查其中的心率、时间等个人信息。

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策。
